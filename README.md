# Raging Studio 🎬

Free tools for filmmakers, in your browser. No account, no subscription, no ads. Your work stays on your computer and belongs to you.

👉 **https://ragingstudio.com**

Available in **English** and **Français**.

## The tools

- **[Raging Page](https://ragingstudio.com/page/)**: screenwriting with industry-standard formatting. Series, structure cards, storyboard, revisions, Final Draft (.fdx), Fountain and PDF.
- **[Raging Draw](https://ragingstudio.com/draw/)**: film posters, designed and customised from A to Z. Templates, 100+ fonts, photos, freehand drawing, PNG / JPEG / PDF export up to 300 dpi.
- More tools are on the way.

## Your data

Everything is saved in your browser, on your computer. No account, no analytics. Google Drive is optional. Note: fonts and PDF tools load from the internet.

## Structure

- `index.html`: the Raging Studio home page
- `page/`: Raging Page
- `draw/`: Raging Draw
- `guide.html`: how the tools work

## Honest warning

100% vibe-coded (built with AI, directed by a filmmaker). It's not perfect and has bugs. That's why it's open source: found a bug or have an idea? Open an [Issue](https://github.com/mtaofficiel69-debug/ragingpage/issues).
